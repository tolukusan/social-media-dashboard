# Implementation work

The completed MVP baseline remains intact. The requested [live integration follow-up](docs/plans/completion/live-integrations.md) now implements Facebook Ads, Instagram Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and Zoho Campaigns setup, mappings, provider clients, and automatic sync. Local verification and remaining limitations are recorded in [integration evidence](docs/evidence/live-integrations-verification.md).

Live activation requires provider access tokens, approved account permissions, compatible reporting-day boundaries, and a real account comparison. Additional providers must be named before their adapters can be implemented. These prerequisites do not prevent preparing connections without keys.

Completed work and verification are recorded in the [completion ledger](docs/plans/completion/README.md) and [implementation progress](docs/evidence/IMPLEMENTATION_PROGRESS.md). Optional and conditional release activities are in the [release checklist](docs/guides/release-checklist.md); the historical import-approval question is tracked in [BLOCKERS](BLOCKERS.md).
